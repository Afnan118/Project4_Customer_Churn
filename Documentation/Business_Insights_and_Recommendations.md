# Day 77: Business Insights & Recommendations

## Project: Customer Churn Analysis & Retention Insights

### Objective

Translate customer churn analysis findings into actionable business insights and customer-retention recommendations.

The goal is to identify high-risk customer segments, understand their potential business impact, and prioritise practical strategies to improve customer retention.

### Tools and Methods

- Python
- Pandas
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Customer Segmentation
- Business Analysis
- Data Visualization

## 1. Key Business Problems

The analysis identified five important customer-retention concerns:

1. High churn among new customers.
2. High churn among month-to-month contract customers.
3. High churn in the month-to-month Fiber optic segment.
4. Higher average monthly charges among churned customers.
5. Shorter average customer tenure among churned customers.

## 2. Business Insights and Recommendations

### 2.1 Improve New Customer Onboarding

**Finding:**

New customers (0–12 months) have a churn rate of 47.44%.

**Business Insight:**

A large proportion of new customers leave during their first year.

**Recommendations:**

- Introduce a structured customer onboarding programme.
- Provide welcome offers and setup assistance.
- Contact new customers during their first three months.
- Resolve early service complaints quickly.

### 2.2 Encourage Longer-Term Contracts

**Finding:**

- Month-to-month: 42.71% churn.
- One-year: 11.27% churn.
- Two-year: 2.83% churn.

**Business Insight:**

Customers on flexible monthly contracts are more likely to leave than customers with longer-term contracts.

**Recommendations:**

- Offer suitable discounts for customers switching to annual contracts.
- Provide additional benefits for longer commitments.
- Explain the value of long-term plans clearly.
- Avoid forcing customers into contracts they do not need.

### 2.3 Investigate Fiber Optic Customer Churn

**Finding:**

Month-to-month Fiber optic customers have the highest observed churn rate at 54.61%.

**Business Insight:**

This customer segment has a particularly high retention risk.

**Recommendations:**

- Investigate internet speed and service reliability complaints.
- Review Fiber optic pricing against competitors.
- Collect feedback from customers considering cancellation.
- Provide faster technical support.

### 2.4 Review Pricing and Customer Value

**Finding:**

- Average monthly charges of stayed customers: ₹61.27.
- Average monthly charges of churned customers: ₹74.44.

**Business Insight:**

Customers who churned paid higher average monthly charges. Pricing and perceived value should be investigated.

**Recommendations:**

- Review pricing across different customer segments.
- Compare service packages with competitor offerings.
- Provide suitable plans based on customer usage.
- Clearly communicate the benefits included in each package.

### 2.5 Strengthen Early Customer Retention

**Finding:**

- Average tenure of stayed customers: 37.57 months.
- Average tenure of churned customers: 17.98 months.

**Business Insight:**

Customers who leave tend to have shorter relationships with the company.

**Recommendations:**

- Introduce retention campaigns for customers during their first year.
- Monitor customer satisfaction regularly.
- Identify customers showing signs of dissatisfaction.
- Follow up on unresolved complaints.

## 3. Prioritisation of Business Actions

| Priority | Business Action | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| High | Investigate Fiber optic monthly customers | Highest churn rate: 54.61% |
| High | Improve new customer onboarding | New customer churn: 47.44% |
| High | Improve month-to-month retention | Churn rate: 42.71% |
| Medium | Review pricing and packages | Higher average charges among churned customers |
| Medium | Improve early retention monitoring | Shorter average tenure among churned customers |

**Prioritisation note:** These priorities are based on observed churn rates and business relevance. Actual resource allocation should also consider segment sizes, revenue, retention costs, and expected financial impact.

## 4. Expected Business Impact

Implementing the recommended retention strategies could help the company:

- Reduce early-stage customer churn through better onboarding.
- Improve customer retention by encouraging suitable long-term contracts.
- Identify and address service issues affecting Fiber optic customers.
- Improve customer satisfaction through better pricing and support.
- Build longer-lasting customer relationships.

These are expected benefits, not measured results. Actual improvements must be evaluated after implementing the recommendations.

## 5. Performance Metrics to Monitor

The company should monitor the following KPIs to evaluate retention efforts:

| KPI | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Overall Churn Rate | Measure the percentage of customers leaving |
| Monthly Churn Rate | Monitor churn trends over time |
| New Customer Churn Rate | Evaluate first-year retention |
| Contract-wise Churn Rate | Compare retention across contract types |
| Customer Tenure | Monitor how long customers stay |
| Average Monthly Charges | Track customer spending trends |
| Retention Rate | Measure the percentage of customers retained |

## 6. Final Business Conclusion

The analysis identified customer churn as an important retention challenge, with an overall churn rate of 26.54%.

New customers, month-to-month contract customers, and month-to-month Fiber optic customers showed particularly high churn rates.

Customers who churned also had higher average monthly charges and shorter average tenure than customers who stayed.

The findings suggest that the company should prioritise early customer onboarding, suitable long-term contract offers, service-quality investigation, and pricing reviews.

By monitoring customer retention KPIs and evaluating the results of these strategies, the company can make more informed decisions to improve customer retention.
