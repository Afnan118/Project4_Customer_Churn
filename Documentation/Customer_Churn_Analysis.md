# Day 74 - Customer Churn Analysis

## 1. Project Objective

To identify customer groups with high churn rates, investigate patterns associated with customer churn, and develop business recommendations to improve customer retention.

## 2. Overall Churn Analysis

Total Customers: 7,043

- Customers who stayed: 5,174 (73.46%)
- Customers who churned: 1,869 (26.54%)

**Finding:** Approximately 1 in 4 customers left the company.

**Recommendation:** The company should investigate customer churn to understand potential issues affecting retention.

## 3. Customer Groups with High Churn

### Contract Type

| Contract | Churn Rate |
|---|---:|
| Month-to-month | 42.71% |
| One year | 11.27% |
| Two year | 2.83% |

**Finding:** Month-to-month customers have the highest churn rate.

**Recommendation:** Investigate the concerns of month-to-month customers and consider suitable retention offers and contract incentives.

### Tech Support

| Tech Support | Churn Rate |
|---|---:|
| No | 41.64% |
| Yes | 15.17% |

**Finding:** Customers without Tech Support have a considerably higher churn rate.

**Recommendation:** Investigate support-related issues and improve technical support availability and service quality.

## 4. Monthly Charges Analysis

| Customer Status | Average Monthly Charges |
|---|---:|
| Stayed | 61.27 |
| Churned | 74.44 |

Difference: 13.18

**Finding:** Customers who churned paid approximately 13.18 more per month on average than customers who stayed.

**Recommendation:** Investigate the pricing structure, compare competitor prices, and review whether customers receive sufficient value for their monthly charges.

## 5. Customer Tenure Analysis

| Customer Status | Average Tenure |
|---|---:|
| Stayed | 37.57 months |
| Churned | 17.98 months |

**Finding:** Customers who churned had a much shorter average tenure than customers who stayed.

**Recommendation:** Investigate the early customer experience and consider introductory offers, better onboarding, and retention support for new customers.

## 6. Key Business Insights

1. The overall customer churn rate is 26.54%.
2. Month-to-month customers have the highest churn rate at 42.71%.
3. Customers without Tech Support have a churn rate of 41.64%.
4. Customers who churned paid higher average monthly charges.
5. Customers who churned had shorter average tenure.

## 7. Business Recommendations

- Investigate why month-to-month customers leave more frequently.
- Improve technical support availability and service quality.
- Review pricing and compare plans with competitors.
- Introduce suitable offers for new customers.
- Improve onboarding and early customer engagement.
- Monitor churn rates regularly to measure the impact of retention strategies.

## 8. Important Limitation

These findings identify associations and patterns in the dataset. They do not prove that pricing, contract type, or support availability directly causes customers to leave. Further investigation, including customer feedback and competitor pricing data, is required.

## 9. Conclusion

The analysis identified several customer groups with higher churn rates and highlighted possible areas for business improvement. The company should prioritise month-to-month customers, investigate support-related issues, review pricing, and improve the early customer experience to support customer retention.
